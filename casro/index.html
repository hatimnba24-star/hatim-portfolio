01 / BRAND ORIGIN

THE ORIGIN &
VISION OF CASRO.

Born in Morocco at the intersection of graphic design, software restraint, and contemporary garment construction.

CASRO StudioCreative Direction by Hatim Drif
"Fashion is architecture in motion — where proportion, weight, and visual silence dictate identity."

Founded in 2024 by creative developer and designer Hatim Drif, CASRO was built to challenge generic streetwear tropes. Instead of excessive branding, CASRO focuses on deliberate cuts, high-density cotton weights, and balanced monochrome iconography.

Every garment, campaign look, and graphic element is crafted like a piece of high-grade software: clean, functional, structured, and resilient against fleeting trends.

Architectural Cut & Sew

Heavyweight fabrics, custom drop-shoulder silhouettes, and tailored boxy cuts engineered for longevity.

Visual Identity Systems

Minimalist typography, vector iconography, and high-contrast colorways designed with intent.

02 / CORE PHILOSOPHY

DESIGN PRINCIPLES
& PILLARS.

01 / STRUCTURE

Architectural Form

We treat garments as structural forms. Every line, shoulder drop, and hem position is calculated to create a balanced silhouette.

02 / RESTRAINT

Minimal Graphic Language

Restraint over noise. Graphics are understated, monochrome, and placed with mathematical precision to maximize visual impact.

03 / TEXTILE

Tactile Quality

Sourcing premium 280GSM+ combed cottons, durable ribbed collars, and soft-touch screen prints built to endure years of wear.

03 / CREATIVE PROCESS

FROM SKETCH TO
FINAL GARMENT.

A rigorous multi-step methodology bridging digital prototyping and physical craftsmanship.

STEP 01

Concept & Vector Drafting

Digital rendering in Figma and Illustrator to balance graphic scales, typography ratios, and print coordinates.

STEP 02

Pattern & Sample Crafting

Developing physical samples, testing neckband tensions, sleeve lengths, and garment drape across body profiles.

STEP 03

Campaign & Lookbook

High-fashion editorial photography, campaign styling, and interactive web showcases to launch the collection.
